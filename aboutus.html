About Us

The Water Project, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization unlocking human potential
by providing reliable water projects to communities in sub-Saharan Africa who
suffer needlessly from a lack of access to clean water and proper sanitation.

Since 2006, we have been helping communities gain access to clean, safe water by
providing training, expertise and financial support for water project construction
through our staff and implementing partners.

Join our community of world-changers, investing in
local teams. Make The Water Promise!
